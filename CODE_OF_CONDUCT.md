# Community conduct

NorthBots welcomes people who want to build useful, understandable agent tools. Help others participate by being respectful, specific and patient.

- Critique ideas and code; avoid personal attacks.
- Credit other people's work and respect their time.
- Keep discussions relevant and allow room for different experience levels.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or repeatedly target another participant.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish someone else's private information, credentials or conversations.
- Do not use project channels for spam, deception or unwanted promotion.

This applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, wiki and Discussions. Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate content, close disruptive threads or restrict participation to protect the community. Apply these standards fairly and focus on the behavior involved.

For private conduct concerns, use a contact option on the [maintainer's profile](https://github.com/matnorte). If no private channel is available, request one publicly without identifying affected people or posting sensitive details. Report software vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). There is no guaranteed response time.
